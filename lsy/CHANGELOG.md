## [Unreleased]

### Added
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-bili` and `@deepseek-ai/dsh-tool-bili`: the agent lives on its own Bilibili account. The owner signs it in once from **Settings → Bilibili account** (B站账号) by scanning a web QR code with the Bilibili app (the international app's Google-sign-in account works; bilibili.com has no Google login); the cookies (SESSDATA, bili_jct, DedeUserID, DedeUserID__ckMd5, sid, buvid3, buvid4) and the refresh token are one `grant` record in the credential store (`bili/account`), never logged, returned to the model, or shown in Settings. A minute after start and daily, `cookie/info` decides whether to refresh: RSA-OAEP (SHA-256) correspond path, `refresh_csrf`, `cookie/refresh`, store, then `confirm/refresh` with the old token; a failed refresh or a dead session marks the account login-needed, stops writes, and sends one WeChat notice (`dsh 的 B站登录失效了：设置 → B站账号 扫码`). Every request goes through one client: an exact method+host+path allowlist (patterns only for the correspond page and subtitle files), no redirects, cookies only to Bilibili API hosts, WBI signing (tested with the documented vector), at least 1.2 s plus jitter between requests, deadlines, lossless big integers. Risk control (HTTP 412/429, codes -352/-412/-509/-799, captcha codes and vouchers) backs reads off 30 minutes and pauses writes persistently until the owner presses Resume in Settings; the model cannot lift a pause, and the owner hears once. Writes pass one host-side guard: `nav` identity check, daily caps (follow 10, unfollow 10, favorite 20, like 30, watch later 30, comment 3, reply 5, dynamic 1, owner DM 5; hard maxima in code), one comment per video and one reply per comment ever, idempotent pre-reads, a random 60-180 s gap (a call waits up to 200 s or answers "try later"), and a durable ledger (`reserved` → `accepted` | `failed` | `uncertain`; uncertain counts and is never retried). An accepted comment not visible to a signed-out visitor five minutes later pauses public speech. Messages go only to `ownerMid` (200000001) and only between mutual followers. A body clock (one random wake in 12:00–14:00 and 19:30–23:30 New York time, 15 % skip chance, plans stored so a restart never wakes twice) and a zero-token inbox poll (every 10 minutes; new replies, mentions, and the owner's messages, at most 2 wakes an hour, as untrusted data) prompt the account's long-lived Session (`/Users/dsh-runtime/bili`, preset `bili`), and its final answers go to WeChat unless `NO_REPLY`. The tools (feeds, search, videos through subtitles with coverage notes, comments, inbox, account; follow, favorite, like, watch later, comment, reply, text dynamic, owner DM; a journal of up to 8 entries of 600 characters a day) wrap everything Bilibili users wrote as `<bilibili_data trust="untrusted">` and cut it at 12,000 characters, and their descriptions ask for rare speech and never anything about the owner. The event bundle mounts `bili` at the root and a new preset `bili` with only a persona, the time context, the tools, and compaction (no shell, files, schedules, monitors, subagents, web, or owner memory); `setup-profile.sh` links both packages and `check-setup-profile.sh` checks them. Uploads are not implemented yet. A market search on 10-23 reviewed nullifyi/dsh-bili-pod, InariNGT-Ofiicial/dsh-bili-humanism, and c1overzz/dsh-bili-scroll (all MIT) and used them as references only: their gates are files an agent with bash can read or forge, dsh-bili-scroll serves unauthenticated routes (an unauthenticated `POST` of its config spawns processes, a remote code execution), and none follows, likes, adds to watch later, or refreshes cookies. Their send state machine, allowlist and brake, and visibility check are credited in the package READMEs.
- WeChat quotes of the bot's own messages reach the agent with their text: iLink delivers such a quote as times only (`create_time_ms`, `update_time_ms`, or the time in `msg_id`'s high bits), so the channel keeps every accepted text (`sent` table, newest 200, 30 days) and matches the nearest send within 15 s; an unmatched quote reads `（一条较早的消息，内容取不到）` instead of vanishing. Follows xmanrui/dsh-im (MIT). A market search for WeChat channels (2026-10-23; topic `dsh-plugin`, 30+ hits) reviewed xmanrui/dsh-im, pan17/dsh-wechat, and gnulife/dsh-plugin-wechat, all REFERENCE or SKIP: unauthenticated routes (dsh-im's delivery route; pan17's config route can redirect the bot token), no `lsyNotifier`, no reply-quota accounting or outbox, and gnulife's cannot load on this fork.
- WeChat feedback while the agent works: the chat shows WeChat's typing line from the moment the owner's message arrives until the reply has gone out, following the OpenClaw plugin's protocol (`ilink/bot/getconfig` typing ticket, cached a day with backoff on failure; `ilink/bot/sendtyping` status 1 every 5 s, status 2 after the reply; at most an hour). It covers every turn of the chat Session, messages joining a running turn, `NO_REPLY` and failed turns, and a forward that starts no turn (cleared after a minute); typing failures are logged once and never touch a reply. New commands: `/stop` cancels every running root turn on the Host (the chat and bus Sessions, `agent.cancel({ kind: 'user' })`), drops their queued messages, and answers with what it stopped, while monitors and reminders keep running; `/help` lists `/stop`, `/new`, and `/help`. Commands are case-insensitive and never reach the agent. The channel also sends OpenClaw's `notifystart` / `notifystop` lifecycle notices. OpenClaw's tool-call progress items were not adopted: each is a `sendmessage`, and whether it counts against the reply window's quota is undocumented.
- WeChat time awareness: when the chat was quiet for `timeGapMs` (30 minutes) or longer, the owner's next message reaches the chat Session right after a notice saying when it was sent, how long the chat was quiet, what the last message was (the agent's reply, another Session's note, a file, a digest) and when, and when the owner last wrote, and that it starts a new exchange unless it refers back. A new `activity` table in the `lsy_wechat` domain records the owner's last message and the bot's last sent message with its kind; it is read before the outbox flush, so a digest the new message releases does not count, and it survives restarts. `0` turns the notice off. The upstream `time-context` reading (mounted) already gave the elapsed time; the market's `xfgil/dsh-session-clock` (MIT, 7d22e0c; unauthenticated `webServer` routes including `POST /toggle`, and blind to notes from other Sessions), `john-walks-slow/dsh-message-datetime`, and `cuteG41cute/dsh-time-stamp` (both repeat `time-context`) were reviewed and not installed.
- Disk-full warnings: `lsy/deploy/disk-watch.mjs`, run by AMS as dsh-runtime (no launchd job of its own), checks the internal Data volume and `/Volumes/MiniSSD` every 5 minutes with statfs and, above 95 %, pushes `host.disk.full` through lsy-event-ingest's new `host` source (`LSY_INGEST_TOKEN_HOST`). The `host-alerts` subscription sends it to WeChat as a plain note (no model); the key carries the volume and the day, so one warning per volume per day.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-lsy-sysmon`: **Settings → System status** (系统状态) shows the Mac's CPU usage, cores, and load averages; memory and swap; whole-machine, CPU, GPU, and ANE watts with die temperatures; and one bar per volume (Internal, MiniSSD) with used, total, and free space, amber from 85% and red from 95%. The section polls its Host route `GET /api/lsy.sysmon` (behind the `/api` sign-in) every 5 s while it is open and the page is visible, and keeps the last reading with a quiet stale line when a read fails. The Host samples on demand (2 s cache, one sample in flight): `os.cpus()` tick deltas, `statfs` per configured mount (an unreadable one is listed with the reason), and macmon (`/Users/dsh-runtime/bin/macmon`, no sudo) run with fixed arguments through `execFile`. Unknown readings are `null`, never `0`, and without macmon the Power card shows `brew install macmon`. A market search found 13+ dsh system monitors and none fit: their routes have no sign-in check, and none reads macOS disks per volume or power; MM998LI/dsh-dock-vitals, weihaobin0818-lgtm/dsh-system-monitor, and vladkens/macmon (all MIT) are cited as references. The section registers into upstream's `settings.section` list, so the only upstream edits are the usual wiring (ledger).
- Workflow skill `dsh-plugin-sourcing` (`.agents/skills/`, read by Claude Code and dsh agents): before building a capability, search this repo and the GitHub topic `dsh-plugin` (the source of truth; market indexes, searched by `scripts/search-market.mts` after `parseMarketIndex` validation, are its mirrors); download candidates with `npm pack` / `git clone` and review them without running anything; then install the reviewed version (`dsh plugin --profile <p> add`), reference it in our own package, or build from scratch, record the decision, and tag anything we publish with the `dsh-plugin` topic.
- `operator` channel: lsy-event-ingest takes a second source, `operator` (`LSY_INGEST_TOKEN_OPERATOR`, readable only by the owner's account), and the bus subscription of the same name starts an unattended Session for each task the owner's own Claude Code sessions push. A new `session.trustedText` flag (host config, off by default) makes the Session prompt present the event text as the owner's request instead of untrusted data; only `operator` sets it.
- lsy-event-bus reports turns that a `lsy-tool-monitor` notice wakes, like reminder turns (`📡 Monitor mon-… (description)`), so a monitor started in a bus-started Session reaches the owner; the WeChat chat Session relays its own turns as before.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-lsy-event-ingest`: `POST /hooks/lsy` lets the owner's own services push events into the event bus. Each source (`{ id, tokenEnv }`) has its own bearer token in the credential store, and the matching token, never the body, decides the event's `source`: `kind` must be dotted lowercase words starting with `<source>.`, a body that names `source` or `actor` is refused, `github` and `schedule` are reserved ids, and every field is bounded. The reply is 202 with the bus outcome (`accepted`, `duplicate`, `suppressed`, or `unmatched`), or 400, 401, 405, 413, 415, or 503 with `{ error }`; `key` is the dedupe key, so a retry never doubles an event. The event bundle mounts it with one source, `kaorou` (`LSY_INGEST_TOKEN_KAOROU` in `$DSH_HOME/.env`), and a `kaorou` bus subscription (`kaorou.*`, `session`, 60 s coalescing, 4 per hour) that starts a diagnosis-only Session against kaorou's API at 127.0.0.1:8096.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-lsy-tool-monitor`: the agent watches anything a shell command can print, as Claude Code's Monitor does. `monitor_start({ command, description, timeout_minutes?, persistent? })` runs `bash -c` in its own process group in the Session's working directory, and each stdout line comes back to the same Session as a `lsy-tool-monitor` notice: an idle agent is woken with `followup()`, a busy one gets it with `inject()`. The Host, not the model, enforces the limits: lines within 500 ms share one notice (at most 50 lines of 2000 characters), a monitor that sends 30 notices within a rolling hour is stopped with an ended notice, the timeout defaults to 60 minutes (at most 1440), and a Session runs at most 10. stderr (the last 10 lines) goes into the ended notice; `monitor_stop` and `monitor_list` manage them. A host restart starts each running monitor again under the same id (see Fixed). The `unattended` preset mounts it and its persona mentions `monitor_start`.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-lsy-memory`: the agent remembers the owner across Sessions. Entries (one dated line each, in claude.ai's categories: instructions, preferences, identity, career, projects) live in `<DSH_HOME>/lsy-memory/memory.jsonl`. The Host, not the model, puts a memory block in front of each root Session, and again after compaction hides it: instructions and preferences in full, career and project entries by their first 80 characters, identity entries (people, family, accounts) only as a count, within `maxBytes` (64 KiB). The agent reads full entries with `memory_search` and writes with `memory_save` and `memory_forget`; the store validates every field, dates entries itself, serializes writes per file, replaces the file atomically, refuses to rewrite a file with an unreadable line, and logs ids, never text. `lsy/deploy/memory-import.mjs` imports a claude.ai memory export with its dates (RUNBOOK step 12); the owner's import dropped ten instructions that only apply to claude.ai's own tools. The `unattended` preset mounts it; subagents get no block.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-lsy-tool-wechat`: the agent sends a local file to the owner on WeChat with `wechat_send_file({ path })`: image bytes as a picture, `.mp4`/`.mov`/`.m4v` as a video, anything else as a file. `WechatChannel.sendFile` enforces everything in host code: the recipient is always the owner, the file goes only through the open reply window (never the outbox) and leaves its last slot for the text reply, and the path must name a non-empty regular file within `maxMediaBytes`. Upload follows the iLink flow (`getuploadurl`, AES-128-ECB, CDN POST, `x-encrypted-param`, `sendmessage`) as in xiaoshihou514/dsh-weixin (MIT) and @tencent-weixin/openclaw-weixin; a test decrypts an upload with the package's own download path.
- GitHub webhooks: `lsy-github-sensor` registers a webhook rule that maps verified GitHub App deliveries into bus events (a published release, a created tag, and default-branch commits with the same keys polling uses, so both paths dedupe; pull requests, issues, comments, reviews, finished workflow runs, and other repository events as `github.<event>.<action>`). The event bundle mounts `@deepseek-ai/dsh-webhook-github` at `/hooks/github` with the secret `LSY_GITHUB_WEBHOOK_SECRET`; GitHub reaches it through the `github-relay` service on phm, which forwards over the tailnet (RUNBOOK step 11). Events arrive in seconds instead of within a minute and cost no REST quota, and polling stays as the fallback.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-lsy-balance`: a card under the DeepSeek account card in **Settings → Account and balance** listing every provider key the Host holds: what an OpenRouter key has left under its limit, a DeepSeek API key's wallets (left out when they repeat the signed-in account), and OpenCode Go's quota windows as bars; exhausted routes turn red, unreadable ones say why. Its Host route `/api/lsy.balance` reads the keys, which never leave the Host (cached a minute). The card fills a new `settings.account.balances` slot of the upstream Account section (ledger). It started as a composer dock pill; the owner moved balances to the Account page. It is the fork's first browser package, so `tsconfig.client.json` gains a reference (ledger).

- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-lsy-github-sensor`: polls releases, tags, branch commits, and `/notifications` every 60 s with `ETag`/`Last-Modified` conditional requests, baselines on first poll, honors `X-Poll-Interval`, pauses on primary and secondary rate limits, persists cursors, retries items whose publish failed, and polls repositories the agent adds at run time (with a repository probe). Includes a fake GitHub REST double and an end-to-end pipeline test.

### Changed
- WeChat splits long answers along Markdown structure: a part ending inside a code fence closes it and the next reopens it with the same opening line (a fence that would be empty moves on whole), and a part inside a table repeats the header and separator rows; blank lines no longer start a part. Follows pan17/dsh-wechat (MIT). A `ret -2` is logged with the text's characters, bytes, and the window's send count, to tell a too-long message from a used-up window.
- The provider balance card shows each OpenCode Go quota window's own reset time (`今天 13:49`, `明天 20:00`, `11-15 13:32`, also in the meter's `aria-valuetext`); the header still names the busiest window. Before, only that window's reset showed, so the monthly window, which rolls on the subscription date rather than the calendar month, had no visible reset.
- Loop suppression is by identity, not by text the model writes: the event bundle suppresses `dsh-events[bot]`, the GitHub App the agent acts as, and no longer matches the `Dsh-Agent:` marker. The service's git identity is that bot (`346633139+dsh-events[bot]@users.noreply.github.com`), so the agent's commits are suppressed already; its comments and pull requests follow once it uses the App's installation token.
- The WeChat chat Session works in `/Users/dsh-runtime/me`, a clone of the owner's private tracking repository `StevenLi-phoenix/me` (event bundle config; RUNBOOK lists the clone). The WeChat inbox ignores itself through its own `.gitignore`, so attachments never land in a commit.
- Model providers, the default model, and the preview-notice acknowledgement move from the home patch to the owner's profile patch: the template seeds the OpenRouter route and `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash`, and the Web UI can change them. With them in the home patch, the Models page refused to save any provider ("Configuration for \"llm-pi-ai\" is overridden by a home patch"). The home patch keeps only the sign-in cookie lifetime and the disabled DeepSeek key onboarding.
- The event composition is a bundle layer, `@deepseek-ai/dsh-lsy-event-bundle` (`lsy/bundles/event/`), listed after the Web bundles in the profile manifest. The profile patch is now the owner's layer: `setup-profile.sh` creates it once from the template, never replaces it, moves a pre-bundle composition copy aside, merges the manifest instead of overwriting it, and links the bundle. Before this, every upgrade replaced the profile patch and dropped whatever the Web UI settings forms had saved there. `check-setup-profile.sh` checks fresh, migrated, and re-run homes and that fresh and migrated homes compose the same; `upgrade.sh` runs it before switching `current`. The composed tree is unchanged (`--dump-config` entries match line for line).
- The home patch also keeps the preview notice acknowledged and turns off the official DeepSeek key onboarding dialog.
- Browser sign-in lasts 365 days: the home patch sets `cookieMaxAgeDays` on the `connection` entry (restating its `trustedHosts`). The cookie signing secret lives in the credential store, so restarts and upgrades keep sessions.
- The event service takes over `dsh.tailc2559b.ts.net` from the interactive instance: the event bus `webUrl` points there, and `RUNBOOK.md` step 10 records the cutover (`svc:dsh` to port 3081, old LaunchAgent stopped and disabled) and its rollback, plus the passwordless operator sudoers entry and live-reload notes.
- Failed turns say why: `failureDetail()` (event bus) turns a `TurnEndReason` of kind `error` into one line (`AUTH: 401 User not found.`, at most 200 characters). The WeChat warning adds `原因：…` under `⚠️ 本轮未完成（error）`, bus result and reminder notes add `Reason: …`, the batch record keeps it as `error`, and both log lines carry it.
- LaunchDaemon `app.lishuyu.dsh-events.plist` also trusts `dsh.tailc2559b.ts.net`, so the event service can take over the Tailscale name of the interactive instance it replaces without a plist change.
- Models route through OpenRouter: `lsy/deploy/home.cordis.patch.yml` (installed as `$DSH_HOME/cordis.patch.yml`) enables the `openrouter` route of `llm-pi-ai` keyed by `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` and makes `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash` the default model. Verified with `--dump-config` and a headless request that reached OpenRouter. `RUNBOOK.md` step 5 now covers the OpenRouter key, one classic GitHub token for both GitHub names, the home patch, and logging `gh` in for `git push` (the harness strips credential-shaped variables from agent shells).
- Event service logs move from `~dsh-runtime/Library/Logs/dsh-events/` (inside the 700 `Library`) to `/Users/dsh-runtime/logs/`, which the operator reads without sudo.

### Fixed
- The Bilibili account's body clock marked a wake that came due before any account was signed in as done, so Settings said it woke (`12:47 已醒`) although the wake was skipped; such a wake is now marked missed.
- Bilibili QR login for the agent's account, which was registered overseas through the international app's Google sign-in: after the scan was confirmed, `qrcode/poll` answered `48007 当前账号暂不支持在本APP登录` because the requests looked like bilibili.com's mainland web version, and the channel polled on until the code expired with the page stuck on "scanned". The login now presents itself as the international web version, as the official login popup does for overseas accounts (`mobi_app: web_i` in `x-bili-device-req-json`, `x-bili-locale-json`, `go_url` and `Referer` `https://www.bilibili.com/en/`, found in `miniLogin.umd.min.js`), and a business code or risk control from the poll ends the attempt with Bilibili's message shown in Settings.
- An unpaired surrogate in an answer (an emoji cut by the model or provider) made `sendmessage` fail; outgoing WeChat text is now made well-formed first.
- A restart (deploy or crash) during a chat turn left the owner's question unanswered with no sign: the channel now sends `⚠️ dsh 正在重启，这一轮被打断了…` while it stops, straight through the last known reply window because storage may already be closed. Turns the owner stopped (Web UI or `/stop`) are no longer reported by the event bus as unfinished, and a chat turn stopped by `/stop` is not relayed as `⚠️ 本轮未完成`.
- A deploy restart (`launchctl kickstart -k`, 2026-10-23 03:09:10) killed the agent's global-awareness monitor `mon-6e3291` without telling it, and cut the turn that monitor had just woken (crash recovery closed it as `interrupted` stamped with its last event's time, so the Web UI showed a 1 ms failed request). lsy-tool-monitor now stores each running monitor (owner, command, description, directory, start, deadline; never the environment) in the `lsy_tool_monitor` domain and, at host start, runs it again with the same id, a freshly scrubbed environment, and the time left to its deadline; a passed deadline or a gone Session drops it. After a restart Sessions load lazily, so a notice for an unloaded owner resolves its agent through the Session controller, and the sweep asks Session persistence instead of the loaded-agent registry, which would have stopped every restored monitor. A `monitor_stop` within the SIGTERM grace before shutdown is no longer undone by the restart.
- The chat Session took the owner's "早上好" at 08:49 for an answer to a disk warning sent at 03:10: the shared copy of another Session's note enters the chat Session's history only at its next step, right before the owner's message, and said it was "just sent". The copy now starts with its send time (`At Thu 2026-10-22 03:10 (America/New_York), another session … sent the owner this message`), and the time-gap notice above names it as the last message.
- The event persona said WeChat shows Markdown literally and asked for no tables or headings; WeChat renders Markdown in bot messages, so the persona and the lsy-wechat README now say so.
- Replies on WeChat to a message another Session sent (an operator task result, a kaorou diagnosis, a monitor or reminder report) reached the chat Session without that message, so the chat agent answered out of context (2026-10-23: "谢谢?" after an operator report got a reply about its own earlier topic). `WechatChannel.send()` now injects each note into the chat Session as context, framed as another Session's message; it waits for the next turn and starts none.
- `lsy/deploy/upgrade.sh` deletes builds older than the one it replaces, keeping that one for a rollback. Old builds (~1.6 GB of `node_modules` each) had been moved to the Trash by hand, which kept their space on the internal disk until it filled on 2026-10-22 and the event service crashed writing its storage.
- A WeChat reply began with `Dsh-Agent: 回复表情包`: the `unattended` persona told the model to mark every "commit or comment" with that trailer, and it treated its reply as a comment. The instruction is gone from the persona (see the identity-based suppression under Changed); nothing model-written decides loop suppression any more.
- WeChat images, files, and videos reach the agent. They were reduced to placeholders such as `[图片]`, so the agent searched the disk for a picture it never had. `lsy-wechat` now downloads each attachment from the WeChat CDN (origin-checked, size-capped, AES-128-ECB decrypted), saves it owner-only in `<workspace>/.wechat-inbox/`, and adds one line per attachment to the prompt; images also go in as image parts, with a text-only retry when the model refuses images. A failed download says the content is unavailable. The layout follows xiaoshihou514/dsh-weixin (MIT). The chat workspace is recreated before each message if it was removed.
- `RUNBOOK.md` step 0: drop the recursive `chown` of `/Volumes/MiniSSD` (with ownership enabled every entry already belonged to `lishuyu`; only the volume root needs it) in favor of a `find` check, stop and restart globalawareness alongside kaorou, and note that `launchctl bootstrap` may report `5: Input/output error` for a service that did start.

### Security
- Model tools mounted at the profile root reach every agent preset, so the `bili` preset's Session (which reads public Bilibili content) would have inherited the base bundle's root `working_directory` tool. The event bundle disables that root row and mounts it in the `unattended` preset; `packages/lsy/bili/tests/bundle.spec.ts` composes the base, Web, and event layers and fails if any model tool is left enabled at the root.
- WeChat login refuses a `redirect_host` or base URL outside `weixin.qq.com` / `wechat.com` over HTTPS on 443: the bot token goes to that host on every later call. Before, any syntactically valid host was followed.
