## [Unreleased]

### Added
- The Bilibili account scouts for the owner (the core loop of c1overzz/dsh-bili-scroll, which we reviewed and did not install, ported into `@deepseek-ai/dsh-bili`).
  - **Keywords and sources.** The owner's search keywords are set in Settings (`关注关键词`, one per line) or in config `keywords`; every wake lists them and asks for a newest-first search (`bili_search` `order: newest` sends `order=pubdate`) before the feeds. The Session can also read the leaderboard (`bili_ranking`, `x/web-interface/ranking/v2`), the hot searches (`bili_hot_searches`, `x/web-interface/wbi/search/square`, as RSSHub's bilibili/hot-search reads it), and a video's danmaku (`bili_danmaku`, 40 lines spread across the video, from `x/v1/dm/list.so`).
  - **Grades and a seen list.** `bili_verdict({ bvid, grade, reason })` grades a watched video `must` (值得看), `ok` (可以看), or `skip` (不值当). The host refuses `must` unless the Session read that video's subtitles with `bili_video`. `bili_seen` reads the grades back, and feeds, search, popular, and the leaderboard mark graded cards with `graded`.
  - **Picks per day and one message per wake.** With `picksPerDay` set (5 in the event bundle), each wake says how many picks went out today and asks for one message carrying this wake's must videos, at most four, each with its title, link, and one line of why.
  - **The watch floor.** `depth.minWatched` (6 in the bundle; 0, the default, holds nothing) and `depth.maxRounds` (3) apply to body-clock and Wake-now turns. A turn that ends before the wake opened that many distinct videos, or that links a video not graded must, is held back (the relay's `deliver` answers `kept`). The host then prompts the same Session to keep browsing, quoting the held answer and naming the ungraded links. After the last continuation, the last answer goes out. Answers to the owner's messages are never held, and a turn the owner stopped ends the wake.
  - **Taste notes.** `bili_taste_write({ text, replaces? })` keeps one 200-character lesson about the owner's taste, up to 20; when full, the Session's oldest note goes, never the owner's, and the Session cannot rewrite the owner's notes. The owner adds and deletes notes in Settings. Every wake and inbox prompt lists them, numbered, with who wrote them.
  - **Finds and how the owner took them.** Each video a delivered message links is kept as a find, with its title (30 kept). The owner's first message within a day after the latest find is kept on it as their reply. Wakes list the last five finds and those replies as untrusted data, and say never to send one again. An owner-message wake names the latest find and asks for a taste note.
  - **Trends.** `bili_trend_write({ term, note, replaces? })` notes a meme or event: what it is, where it comes from, and which community it lives in (60 kept). Wakes list the last 30 days, at most 15. The owner deletes entries in Settings.
  - **Settings.** Four new cards in Settings → Bilibili account: 它刷过的视频 (today's counts by grade, picks against the quota, every graded video with grade filters, a link, its reason, and sent and subtitles-not-read marks), 关注关键词, 它记住的口味 (the notes with add and delete, and the finds with the owner's replies), and 圈内热梗.
  - The feeds, popular, search, and the leaderboard leave out the owner's own uploads: the owner has seen them, and they crowded the account's home feed.
- Quota pace on the provider balance card: each OpenCode Go bar has a tick where its window's time stands (five hours, seven days, or the month since the same day before the reset), and a line under it says how much time is gone (`时间已过 33%`) and, after a tenth of the window with some use, the use at the reset at the pace so far (`照这个速度，到期约用 75%`) or when it runs out first (`照这个速度，明天 10:32 用完`, with the bar in the warning color).
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-owner-relay` (`ctx.ownerRelay`): one relay of a Session's final answer to the owner, replacing the three copies in lsy-wechat, bili, and the bus's ownership check. Channels register (`registerChannel`) and claim their Sessions (`claim`, `release`, `ownerOf`; claims live in memory and each channel claims its stored Sessions again when it registers); at each turn end the relay applies one rule set (aborted with cause `user` or a final `NO_REPLY` sends nothing, a completed turn sends its text, any other end adds `⚠️ 本轮未完成（<kind>）` and `原因：…`) and calls the claiming channel's `deliver`, with `onTurnStart`, `onTurnEnd`, and `onTurnSettled` hooks around it. A channel that cannot deliver falls back to `ctx.lsyNotifier` with its `fallbackLabel` as the note's new `origin`. lsy-wechat registers channel `wechat` (typing and the earlier-chat copy move onto the hooks and its `deliver`), and bili registers `bili-dm`, whose code moves to `src/dm.ts`; the copied `relayBody`, `isSilentReply`, and `failureDetail` in bili and `relayText` in lsy-wechat are gone. Every decision is logged at info as `owner-relay: …` with the Session id and the reason. The event bundle mounts it before the bus.
- Bilibili answers come back on Bilibili: each turn a wake started (body clock, Wake now, inbox) ends with its final answer sent as a private message from the agent's account to `ownerMid` instead of WeChat, unless it is `NO_REPLY`. An answer to the owner's own message is a reply under a new `dmReply` cap (30 a day, hard maximum 60, sent 3-10 s after the previous write, `dmReplyGapMinMs`/`dmReplyGapMaxMs`); any other turn's answer is a share under `dmOwner` (5). Answers are split into at most two 500-character messages, the rest cut with `…`. A message from the owner wakes the Session at once (outside the hourly inbox cap and off-peak rule, at most 30 an hour) and keeps a 60-second message check running until 15 minutes after the latest one. WeChat keeps only what needs the owner: the login, pause, and hidden-comment notices, plus any answer that could not go out as a message (not mutual followers, budget, pause, a failed or uncertain send), titled `📺 B站：这条没能用 B站私信发出，改发微信` with the reason. A turn no wake started (the owner chatting in the Web UI) is not relayed, and neither is a turn the owner's WeChat `/stop` aborted (`aborted` with cause `user`, as the bus and WeChat relays already do). Prompts and the `bili_dm_owner` description say the final answer is the Bilibili message, in plain text with `https://www.bilibili.com/video/<bvid>` links.
- Bilibili phone verification: a write Bilibili refuses until the account binds or verifies a phone number or real name (codes -108, -110, -113, -114, -115, 21015, 4126021, or wording with 手机, 绑定, 实名, or 验证 but not 验证码) is a refusal, not risk control: nothing pauses, the ledger records it `failed` with Bilibili's words, and it is not uncertain, so the same text can go later. An answer that hits it goes to WeChat ending with `B站私信没发出：<Bilibili's words>`, and Settings → Bilibili account shows a lasting hint (`这个号需要验证手机号才能发私信`, or for comments and dynamics) quoting Bilibili until a write of that kind goes through.
- The Bilibili account levels up from what it really does: after `bili_video` returns a subtitle page the host reports one watch of that video as far as it read (`x/click-interface/web/heartbeat`; once per video a day, at most `caps.watch` 10, outside the write gap), and when an answer it delivered to the owner links a video it reports one share (`x/web-interface/share/add`; once per video ever, `caps.share` 5); a failed report is logged and never retried. Coins: `bili_coin({ bvid, count? })` gives 2 coins to a video its uploader marked original and 1 otherwise (or `count`), never with a like, once per video ever (coins given before are read from `x/web-interface/archive/coins`), refused when the balance is short; `bili_triple({ bvid })` (一键三连, `x/web-interface/archive/like/triple`) likes, coins, and saves to the default folder in one request, once per video ever, in the normal write gap, reporting parts already done; the like and favorite it adds count against those caps. Coins have no daily cap, and Bilibili's coin codes (-104, 34002-34005) come back as refusals, never retried. `bili_me` and a new level card in Settings show the level, EXP to the next level, the coin balance, and today's login, watch, share, and coin EXP (`x/member/web/exp/reward`), refreshed with each cookie check and after each report, coin, or triple.
- Bilibili wakes follow model pricing: a new `pricing` setting of `@deepseek-ai/dsh-bili` (empty by default; no schedule in code) lists, per group of providers, peak spans in UTC (`days`, `start`, `end`) and `noPeakDates`, and everything else is off-peak. Before each day's plan and each wake the account reads its Session's route the way the Session Controller does (an explicit pick not yet used, else the last request, else the profile default; the Session is resolved only when one exists). On a priced route the plan clips each `wakes` window (still in `timeZone`, converted to UTC across DST) to its off-peak part and picks a time weighted by length, or moves a window that is all peak to start where that peak ends, keeping its length; a wake or new inbox items that come due in a peak wait for its end and still fire once (yesterday's plan is watched too, so a wake moved past local midnight fires). Unpriced routes wake exactly as before. Each plan logs its route and what was clipped or moved, and Settings → Bilibili account shows the route, whether it is priced (`deepseek-official：按峰谷计价，只在谷时醒` / `opencode-go：不分时段计价`), the next off-peak start during a peak, and which wakes were clipped, moved, or deferred. The event bundle carries DeepSeek's official schedule (api-docs.deepseek.com pricing, in force since 2026-08-17: half price outside Monday–Friday 09:00–12:00 and 14:00–18:00 Beijing time, i.e. 01:00–04:00 and 06:00–10:00 UTC) for `deepseek-official` and `deepseek-account`, with 2027-01-01 as its one listed holiday.
- Bilibili **Wake now** (立即唤醒): a button in Settings → Bilibili account's body-clock card (`POST /api/bili` `{ action: 'wake' }`, behind the sign-in) prompts the account's Session at once with the body-clock prompt, outside today's plan, and records the wake with reason `manual` (手动). It is the owner's request, so it runs in a priced route's peak and during a read back-off and says so; it is refused, with the reason in the view, while signed out, while the login needs renewing, while the Session is in a turn, or within a minute of the last started one. One log line per manual wake.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-bili` and `@deepseek-ai/dsh-tool-bili`: the agent lives on its own Bilibili account. The owner signs it in once from **Settings → Bilibili account** (B站账号) by scanning a web QR code with the Bilibili app (the international app's Google-sign-in account works; bilibili.com has no Google login); the cookies (SESSDATA, bili_jct, DedeUserID, DedeUserID__ckMd5, sid, buvid3, buvid4) and the refresh token are one `grant` record in the credential store (`bili/account`), never logged, returned to the model, or shown in Settings. A minute after start and daily, `cookie/info` decides whether to refresh: RSA-OAEP (SHA-256) correspond path, `refresh_csrf`, `cookie/refresh`, store, then `confirm/refresh` with the old token; a failed refresh or a dead session marks the account login-needed, stops writes, and sends one WeChat notice (`dsh 的 B站登录失效了：设置 → B站账号 扫码`). Every request goes through one client: an exact method+host+path allowlist (patterns only for the correspond page and subtitle files), no redirects, cookies only to Bilibili API hosts, WBI signing (tested with the documented vector), at least 1.2 s plus jitter between requests, deadlines, lossless big integers. Risk control (HTTP 412/429, codes -352/-412/-509/-799, captcha codes and vouchers) backs reads off 30 minutes and pauses writes persistently until the owner presses Resume in Settings; the model cannot lift a pause, and the owner hears once. Writes pass one host-side guard: `nav` identity check, daily caps (follow 10, unfollow 10, favorite 20, like 30, watch later 30, comment 3, reply 5, dynamic 1, owner DM 5; hard maxima in code), one comment per video and one reply per comment ever, idempotent pre-reads, a random 60-180 s gap (a call waits up to 200 s or answers "try later"), and a durable ledger (`reserved` → `accepted` | `failed` | `uncertain`; uncertain counts and is never retried). An accepted comment not visible to a signed-out visitor five minutes later pauses public speech. Messages go only to the owner's main account (its UID set in Settings) and only between mutual followers. A body clock (one random wake in 12:00–14:00 and 19:30–23:30 New York time, 15 % skip chance, plans stored so a restart never wakes twice) and a zero-token inbox poll (every 10 minutes; new replies, mentions, and the owner's messages, at most 2 wakes an hour, as untrusted data) prompt the account's long-lived Session (`/Users/dsh-runtime/bili`, preset `bili`), and its final answers reach the owner unless `NO_REPLY` (as Bilibili messages; see above). The tools (feeds, search, videos through subtitles with coverage notes, comments, inbox, account; follow, favorite, like, watch later, comment, reply, text dynamic, owner DM; a journal of up to 8 entries of 600 characters a day) wrap everything Bilibili users wrote as `<bilibili_data trust="untrusted">` and cut it at 12,000 characters, and their descriptions ask for rare speech and never anything about the owner. The event bundle mounts `bili` at the root and a new preset `bili` with only a persona, the time context, the tools, and compaction (no shell, files, schedules, monitors, subagents, web, or owner memory); `setup-profile.sh` links both packages and `check-setup-profile.sh` checks them. Uploads are not implemented yet. A market search on 10-23 reviewed nullifyi/dsh-bili-pod, InariNGT-Ofiicial/dsh-bili-humanism, and c1overzz/dsh-bili-scroll (all MIT) and used them as references only: their gates are files an agent with bash can read or forge, dsh-bili-scroll serves unauthenticated routes (an unauthenticated `POST` of its config spawns processes, a remote code execution), and none follows, likes, adds to watch later, or refreshes cookies. Their send state machine, allowlist and brake, and visibility check are credited in the package READMEs.
- WeChat quotes of the bot's own messages reach the agent with their text: iLink delivers such a quote as times only (`create_time_ms`, `update_time_ms`, or the time in `msg_id`'s high bits), so the channel keeps every accepted text (`sent` table, newest 200, 30 days) and matches the nearest send within 15 s; an unmatched quote reads `（一条较早的消息，内容取不到）` instead of vanishing. Follows xmanrui/dsh-im (MIT). A market search for WeChat channels (2026-10-23; topic `dsh-plugin`, 30+ hits) reviewed xmanrui/dsh-im, pan17/dsh-wechat, and gnulife/dsh-plugin-wechat, all REFERENCE or SKIP: unauthenticated routes (dsh-im's delivery route; pan17's config route can redirect the bot token), no `lsyNotifier`, no reply-quota accounting or outbox, and gnulife's cannot load on this fork.
- WeChat feedback while the agent works: the chat shows WeChat's typing line from the moment the owner's message arrives until the reply has gone out, following the OpenClaw plugin's protocol (`ilink/bot/getconfig` typing ticket, cached a day with backoff on failure; `ilink/bot/sendtyping` status 1 every 5 s, status 2 after the reply; at most an hour). It covers every turn of the chat Session, messages joining a running turn, `NO_REPLY` and failed turns, and a forward that starts no turn (cleared after a minute); typing failures are logged once and never touch a reply. New commands: `/stop` cancels every running root turn on the Host (the chat and bus Sessions, `agent.cancel({ kind: 'user' })`), drops their queued messages, and answers with what it stopped, while monitors and reminders keep running; `/help` lists `/stop`, `/new`, and `/help`. Commands are case-insensitive and never reach the agent. The channel also sends OpenClaw's `notifystart` / `notifystop` lifecycle notices. OpenClaw's tool-call progress items were not adopted: each is a `sendmessage`, and whether it counts against the reply window's quota is undocumented.
- WeChat time awareness: when the chat was quiet for `timeGapMs` (30 minutes) or longer, the owner's next message reaches the chat Session right after a notice saying when it was sent, how long the chat was quiet, what the last message was (the agent's reply, another Session's note, a file, a digest) and when, and when the owner last wrote, and that it starts a new exchange unless it refers back. A new `activity` table in the `lsy_wechat` domain records the owner's last message and the bot's last sent message with its kind; it is read before the outbox flush, so a digest the new message releases does not count, and it survives restarts. `0` turns the notice off. The upstream `time-context` reading (mounted) already gave the elapsed time; the market's `xfgil/dsh-session-clock` (MIT, 7d22e0c; unauthenticated `webServer` routes including `POST /toggle`, and blind to notes from other Sessions), `john-walks-slow/dsh-message-datetime`, and `cuteG41cute/dsh-time-stamp` (both repeat `time-context`) were reviewed and not installed.
- Disk-full warnings: `lsy/deploy/disk-watch.mjs`, run by AMS as dsh-runtime (no launchd job of its own), checks the internal Data volume and `/Volumes/MiniSSD` every 5 minutes with statfs and, above 95 %, pushes `host.disk.full` through lsy-event-ingest's new `host` source (`LSY_INGEST_TOKEN_HOST`). The `host-alerts` subscription sends it to WeChat as a plain note (no model); the key carries the volume and the day, so one warning per volume per day.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-lsy-sysmon`: **Settings → System status** (系统状态) shows the Mac's CPU usage, cores, and load averages; memory and swap; whole-machine, CPU, GPU, and ANE watts with die temperatures; and one bar per volume (Internal, MiniSSD) with used, total, and free space, amber from 85% and red from 95%. The section polls its Host route `GET /api/lsy.sysmon` (behind the `/api` sign-in) every 5 s while it is open and the page is visible, and keeps the last reading with a quiet stale line when a read fails. The Host samples on demand (2 s cache, one sample in flight): `os.cpus()` tick deltas, `statfs` per configured mount (an unreadable one is listed with the reason), and macmon (`/Users/dsh-runtime/bin/macmon`, no sudo) run with fixed arguments through `execFile`. - `operator` channel: lsy-event-ingest takes a second source, `operator` (`LSY_INGEST_TOKEN_OPERATOR`, readable only by the owner's account), and the bus subscription of the same name starts an unattended Session for each task the owner's own Claude Code sessions push. A new `session.trustedText` flag (host config, off by default) makes the Session prompt present the event text as the owner's request instead of untrusted data; only `operator` sets it.
- lsy-event-bus reports turns that a `lsy-tool-monitor` notice wakes, like reminder turns (`📡 Monitor mon-… (description)`), so a monitor started in a bus-started Session reaches the owner; the WeChat chat Session relays its own turns as before.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-lsy-event-ingest`: `POST /hooks/lsy` lets the owner's own services push events into the event bus. Each source (`{ id, tokenEnv }`) has its own bearer token in the credential store, and the matching token, never the body, decides the event's `source`: `kind` must be dotted lowercase words starting with `<source>.`, a body that names `source` or `actor` is refused, `github` and `schedule` are reserved ids, and every field is bounded. The reply is 202 with the bus outcome (`accepted`, `duplicate`, `suppressed`, or `unmatched`), or 400, 401, 405, 413, 415, or 503 with `{ error }`; `key` is the dedupe key, so a retry never doubles an event. The event bundle mounts it with one source, `kaorou` (`LSY_INGEST_TOKEN_KAOROU` in `$DSH_HOME/.env`), and a `kaorou` bus subscription (`kaorou.*`, `session`, 60 s coalescing, 4 per hour) that starts a diagnosis-only Session against kaorou's API at 127.0.0.1:8096.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-lsy-tool-monitor`: the agent watches anything a shell command can print, as Claude Code's Monitor does. `monitor_start({ command, description, timeout_minutes?, persistent? })` runs `bash -c` in its own process group in the Session's working directory, and each stdout line comes back to the same Session as a `lsy-tool-monitor` notice: an idle agent is woken with `followup()`, a busy one gets it with `inject()`. The Host, not the model, enforces the limits: lines within 500 ms share one notice (at most 50 lines of 2000 characters), a monitor that sends 30 notices within a rolling hour is stopped with an ended notice, the timeout defaults to 60 minutes (at most 1440), and a Session runs at most 10. stderr (the last 10 lines) goes into the ended notice; `monitor_stop` and `monitor_list` manage them. A host restart starts each running monitor again under the same id (see Fixed). The `unattended` preset mounts it and its persona mentions `monitor_start`.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-lsy-memory`: the agent remembers the owner across Sessions. Entries (one dated line each, in claude.ai's categories: instructions, preferences, identity, career, projects) live in `<DSH_HOME>/lsy-memory/memory.jsonl`. The Host, not the model, puts a memory block in front of each root Session, and again after compaction hides it: instructions and preferences in full, career and project entries by their first 80 characters, identity entries (people, family, accounts) only as a count, within `maxBytes` (64 KiB). The agent reads full entries with `memory_search` and writes with `memory_save` and `memory_forget`; the store validates every field, dates entries itself, serializes writes per file, replaces the file atomically, refuses to rewrite a file with an unreadable line, and logs ids, never text. `lsy/deploy/memory-import.mjs` imports a claude.ai memory export with its dates (RUNBOOK step 12); the owner's import dropped ten instructions that only apply to claude.ai's own tools. The `unattended` preset mounts it; subagents get no block.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-lsy-tool-wechat`: the agent sends a local file to the owner on WeChat with `wechat_send_file({ path })`: image bytes as a picture, `.mp4`/`.mov`/`.m4v` as a video, anything else as a file. `WechatChannel.sendFile` enforces everything in host code: the recipient is always the owner, the file goes only through the open reply window (never the outbox) and leaves its last slot for the text reply, and the path must name a non-empty regular file within `maxMediaBytes`. Upload follows the iLink flow (`getuploadurl`, AES-128-ECB, CDN POST, `x-encrypted-param`, `sendmessage`) as in xiaoshihou514/dsh-weixin (MIT) and @tencent-weixin/openclaw-weixin; a test decrypts an upload with the package's own download path.

The event bundle mounts `@deepseek-ai/dsh-webhook-github` at `/hooks/github` with the secret `LSY_GITHUB_WEBHOOK_SECRET`; GitHub reaches it through the `github-relay` service on phm, which forwards over the tailnet (RUNBOOK step 11). ### Security
- The owner's Bilibili UID sat in this public repository: the event bundle's `bili` row, the dsh-bili README examples, and its tests, which tied the owner's GitHub account to their Bilibili account. It now lives only in the runtime's storage: **Settings → Bilibili account → Main account** sets it (the configured `ownerMid` remains a fallback for setups that keep it out of committed files), the bundle carries none and a test keeps it that way, the tests use a made-up UID, and `lsy/main` was rewritten to remove it from history. The `bili` persona no longer names the owner's account either.
- Model tools mounted at the profile root reach every agent preset, so the `bili` preset's Session (which reads public Bilibili content) would have inherited the base bundle's root `working_directory` tool. The event bundle disables that root row and mounts it in the `unattended` preset; `packages/lsy/bili/tests/bundle.spec.ts` composes the base, Web, and event layers and fails if any model tool is left enabled at the root.
- WeChat login refuses a `redirect_host` or base URL outside `weixin.qq.com` / `wechat.com` over HTTPS on 443: the bot token goes to that host on every later call. Before, any syntactically valid host was followed.
